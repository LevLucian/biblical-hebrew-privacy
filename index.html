Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Biblical Hebrew ("the app") is a study tool for Biblical Hebrew vocabulary and the Hebrew alphabet. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

Summary

The app collects nothing, sends nothing anywhere, and has no way to identify you. Everything the app stores lives only on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Vocabulary and your study progress (which words you've marked correct or incorrect, when, and which you've flagged for extra review) are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage frameworks (SwiftData and UserDefaults).
  • This data never leaves your device. The app makes no network connections, has no server, and has no way to transmit your data anywhere.
  • There is no account, sign-up, or login of any kind.

What the app does not do

  • Does not collect personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • Does not use analytics or crash-reporting services
  • Does not show ads or use ad-tracking
  • Does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • Does not share data with any third party, because no data is collected to share
  • Does not use any third-party SDKs

Children's privacy

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children, so it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

Deleting your data

Because all data stays on your device, uninstalling the app permanently deletes it. There is nothing stored anywhere else to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that uses the network — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
